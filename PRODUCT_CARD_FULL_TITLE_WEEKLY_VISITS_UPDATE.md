# Product card full-title and weekly-visit update

- Widened the All Products catalog container while keeping three cards per desktop row.
- Removed title truncation so complete product names are shown.
- Reduced product-title font weight for a friendlier card.
- Kept genuine customization options, ratings/review count, starting price, bulk pricing, favorite control, primary CTA, and details link.
- Removed manual shopper-activity fallback.
- Product cards now show unique shoppers who visited the product details page during the last seven days.
- Product listing impressions no longer count as product visits.
- Added `viewed_detail_at` to separate genuine detail visits from older card-impression records.
