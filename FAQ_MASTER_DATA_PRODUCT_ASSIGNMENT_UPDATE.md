# FAQ Master Data and Product Assignment Update

- Product FAQs are now reusable Master Data records.
- Added Master Data → FAQs CRUD with search, status, ordering, product usage count, and safe deletion.
- Existing product-specific FAQs are migrated into reusable FAQ records and their product assignments are preserved.
- Product add/edit pages now select FAQs instead of creating inline FAQ content.
- FAQ edits automatically affect every assigned product; inactive FAQs are hidden on the storefront.
- Product duplication preserves FAQ assignments.
- SKU is displayed beneath the Publish heading.
- Header navigation spacing and USA Shipping typography are aligned with other menu items.
