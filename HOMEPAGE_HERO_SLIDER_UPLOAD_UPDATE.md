# Homepage Hero Slider Upload Update

The homepage Hero Banner editor now manages the images used by the right-side hero carousel.

- Open **Admin → Homepage Sections → Hero Banner**.
- Use **Hero Image Slider** to upload, reorder, replace, or remove images.
- Recommended image size: **1200 × 820 px**.
- Recommended aspect ratio: **60:41** (approximately **1.46:1**).
- Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF, up to 10 MB per image.
- Important subjects should stay near the center to allow responsive edge cropping.

The images are stored on the public disk under `homepage/sections/hero/slides` and are rendered dynamically on the storefront.
