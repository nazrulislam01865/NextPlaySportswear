# Amazon-inspired wide storefront update

- Increased the shared desktop storefront and header layout ceiling from 1180/1320px to 1800px.
- Added responsive page gutters so the layout stays aligned at 1024px, 1440px, 1920px, and ultrawide resolutions.
- Expanded product catalogs to 4 or 5 columns depending on available width and filter-sidebar presence.
- Expanded homepage product rows and latest-product sliders to 5 cards on wide desktops and up to 6 cards on ultrawide displays.
- Kept tablet and mobile layouts unchanged at two and one columns.
- Kept related/recommended cards at a controlled maximum width so a short row does not become oversized.
- Widened the homepage hero and shared home sections while retaining the existing typography and component design.
- Widened header, utility bar, navigation panel, content sections, and footer to the same alignment system.
