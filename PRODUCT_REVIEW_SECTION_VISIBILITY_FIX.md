# Product Review Section Visibility Fix

- The product details review section no longer disappears when imported product review data is absent.
- The section uses the same configured rating/review-count source as storefront product cards so both views remain consistent.
- Genuine written product reviews continue to render as review cards when present in the product data.
- When written reviews are not available, the review area stays visible and shows an appropriate empty state instead of removing the full section.
- Related products remain directly below the review section.
